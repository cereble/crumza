Components

The Crumza kit, in the Mochi brand until you pick another below. No borders and no shadows: every layer is paper with a little more or less ink in it. Everything moves on real springs: one plate glides between choices and stretches as it goes, overlays grow out of what opened them, small things pop, and keyboard changes land at once.
